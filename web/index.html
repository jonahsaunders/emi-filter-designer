    Operating points (noise model)
    
        
    
        
    Model notes
    
        
      
          
    • DM noise: converter input current (trapezoid for pulsed-input topologies, triangle for boost/PFC) injected across the converter input capacitor. CM noise: switch-node voltage coupled to chassis/PE through Cp.
      • 
          
    • Each switching harmonic is solved with a full nodal analysis including the artificial network(s), capacitor ESR/ESL, inductor DCR/SRF, CM-choke leakage, and MLCC DC-bias derating. DM and CM contributions are added in magnitude (worst case) per line.
      • 
          
    • Narrow-band harmonics read the same on PK, QP and AV detectors, so the AV limit is used as the design target by default.
      • 
          
    • Layout (loop areas, near-field coupling, cable resonances) is not modelled – keep margin and verify with a pre-compliance scan.
